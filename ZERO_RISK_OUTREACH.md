# Dunnify — Zero-Risk Customer Outreach Playbook

> **Goal:** Secure our first 5–10 active Stripe merchants before public launch.  
> **Offer Structure:** 100% Free Recovery for the first $1,000 in failed payments. Zero upfront cost.  
> **Target Audience:** Bootstrapped SaaS Founders, Digital Agencies, and High-Ticket Subscription Creators.

---

## 1. The Strategy: Complete Risk Reversal

### Why Traditional Cold Outreach Fails
Most software founders send cold messages saying:
> *"Hey, check out our new tool! Can we jump on a 20-minute Zoom call so I can show you a demo?"*

This fails 99% of the time because:
1. Founders are busy and hate demo calls.
2. They don't know you or trust your brand yet.
3. They don't want to add another $100/mo subscription to their credit card.

### The Dunnify "Free Money" Angle
Instead of asking for their time or money, **we offer them free cash with zero risk**:
* **No demo calls required:** 60-second setup via a Stripe webhook.
* **No credit card needed:** 100% free until we recover $1,000 of their lost revenue.
* **Pure upside:** If we recover $1,000, they keep 100% of the money. If we fail, they delete the webhook and lost nothing.
* **Our only ask:** If we put $1,000 back in their Stripe account, they give us a 1-sentence honest testimonial and a logo for our website.

* **Where to find them:** LinkedIn (Search: "Founder at Webflow Agency", "Founder at Shopify Agency", "CEO at Design Agency").
* **Their pain:** High-ticket clients who silently miss invoice renewals over expired cards. A single failed charge is a $2,000 loss.

### Segment C: Paid Community & Course Creators (Skool, Circle, Whop)
* **Where to find them:** Skool leaderboards, Twitter creator networks.
* **Their pain:** Members constantly hit bank declines or card limits. Email notices get ignored, resulting in high monthly drop-off.

## 4. How to Handle the Top 4 Objections

### Objection 1: "Is my Stripe account secure? Do I have to give you full admin access?"
* **Our Response:** *"Zero admin access required. You never give us your master password or banking details. You simply paste our Webhook URL into your Stripe dashboard with restricted read-only permissions for `invoice.payment_failed`. You can revoke it at any second."*

### Objection 2: "Will my customers get annoyed receiving a WhatsApp message?"
* **Our Response:** *"The message is designed as a helpful customer service notice, not a sales blast: 'Hi [Name], your payment for [AppName] had a temporary bank decline. Tap here to update in 10s with Apple Pay to keep your service active.' Customers actually appreciate it because it saves them from getting locked out of their account."*

### Objection 3: "I already have Stripe's automated emails turned on."
* **Our Response:** *"Keep them turned on! Dunnify doesn't replace Stripe emails—it supplements them. Email open rates are ~18%, while WhatsApp open rates are 95%. Think of Dunnify as an extra safety net that catches the 80% of customers who never open billing emails."*

### Objection 4: "What happens after you recover the first $1,000 for free?"
* **Our Response:** *"Once you see the first $1,000 back in your Stripe account, if you love the results, Dunnify is just $79/month. If you don't want to continue, you can disconnect with 1 click and keep the $1,000 for free."*
